The import also reads the isolated effect dimensions and supporting animation
tables from battle-animations.ts at the same revision:
https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client/blob/f49fca99f83d7b61495c45274092de0dfe7de071/play.pokemonshowdown.com/src/battle-animations.ts
The source header identifies the standalone replay/animation engine as MIT
and most resource definitions and miscellaneous animation data as CC0.
The MIT permission notice appears below for any MIT-covered material used.

Pocket Mappori records move timings, paths, and aliases at build preparation in
scripts/generate-showdown-battle-animations.mjs. Generated timelines are kept in
src/battle/fx/showdown-data.json. Local Canvas artwork supplies all effects and
backdrops. This import includes no upstream effect images, Pokémon sprites,
background images, audio, or client runtime. The upstream project's repository-
wide AGPL licensing does not change the licensing of the isolated material
identified above.
