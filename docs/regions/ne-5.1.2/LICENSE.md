Made with Natural Earth. Data are in the public domain, including modification and electronic redistribution. No additional restriction is imposed on this derived data.

Derived by Pocket Wander with `scripts/build-encounter-regions.mjs`. Source URLs, exact SHA256 checksums, original sizes, transformed sizes, and transformation precision are in `manifest.json`.

These generalized 1:10m boundaries are approximate, reflect Natural Earth's default de facto representation, and are neither survey-grade nor an assertion about disputed sovereignty. Administrative structures and translated names can be incomplete or outdated. See the official source descriptions for limitations.
